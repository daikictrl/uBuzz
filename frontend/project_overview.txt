================================================================================
                                U-BUZZ MOBILE APP
                                PROJECT OVERVIEW
================================================================================

1. INTRODUCTION & PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
U-Buzz is a campus-focused, short-form video sharing mobile application designed
specifically for students. Think of it as a private campus version of TikTok or 
Instagram Reels. It allows students within a university community to record, 
upload, discover, and watch vertical full-screen videos, connect with peers, 
and share campus moments. 


2. DESIGN & FIRST IMPRESSIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The app uses a premium, dark-themed aesthetic with vibrant accents (deep blues, 
purples, and energetic pinks). 

*   Glassmorphism: The interfaces feature modern, semi-transparent "glass-like" 
    cards that overlay colorful gradient backgrounds, creating a polished, 
    floating look.
*   Smooth Layout Transitions: When loading contents, the app displays soft 
    loading "skeletons" (grey placeholder shapes that pulse gently) instead of 
    abrupt loading screens, making the app feel incredibly responsive.


3. GETTING STARTED (SIGN-UP & LOGIN)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To use the app, students must create a verified account:

*   Form Information: Students sign up using their Full Name, a unique 
    @username, their student Email, and a password.
*   Matricule (Student ID Number): To ensure the community is restricted to 
    real students, users must provide their student registration number 
    (Matricule) during sign-up. 
*   Privacy Protection (Masking): To protect student identity and data privacy, 
    their student registration number is automatically masked when shown to 
    other users (for example, "IU20241234" is displayed to others as "IU****34").
*   Email Verification: After registering, the app sends a verification email. 
    Users must confirm their email address before they can log in.
*   Safe Auto-Logouts: If a student's session expires due to inactivity, the 
    app securely logs them out and redirects them back to the login screen with 
    a message, rather than crashing or freezing.


4. NAVIGATION (THE BOTTOM TAB MENU)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Navigation is simple and intuitive, utilizing a bottom menu bar with four slots:

1.  Home Feed (House Icon): The main screen to view full-screen videos.
2.  Search (Magnifying Glass Icon): The search screen to find other students.
3.  Upload (Raised Purple "+" Button): A prominent button positioned in the 
    middle of the bar that opens the camera/video selection screen.
4.  Profile (Person Icon): The gateway to your own profile details, video grid, 
    settings, and activity indicators.


5. CORE FEATURES & FUNCTIONALITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. THE HOME VIDEO FEED
The primary screen where students watch vertical, full-screen videos. 
*   Paging Control: Swiping up or down snaps perfectly to the next or previous 
    video.
*   Two Content Feeds:
    *   "For You" Feed: Shows videos uploaded by any student on campus.
    *   "Following" Feed: Shows videos exclusively from accounts that you follow.
*   Smart Playback: Videos automatically play when visible and loop indefinitely. 
    If you leave the screen (e.g. to search, open a sheet, or upload), the video 
    automatically pauses to save your phone's battery and mobile data.
*   Global Volume Sync (Mute Toggle): A volume icon sits in the top-right 
    corner of the video player. Tapping it mutes or unmutes the audio. The app 
    remembers this volume preference and applies it to every video you scroll 
    to next.
*   Smart Caption Expansion: Captions appear at the bottom-left. If a student 
    writes a long caption, it is neatly truncated with a purple "more" link. 
    Tapping "more" expands the caption in a scrollable, transparent container 
    so you can read the whole text without covering the buttons. Tapping "less" 
    collapses it back.
*   Video Options: Pressing and holding on a video brings up options to report 
    inappropriate content or delete the post (if it belongs to you).

B. VIDEO INTERACTIONS & LIKES
*   Double-Tap to Like: Tapping quickly twice anywhere on the video screen 
    triggers a floating red "heart burst" animation at the tap location and 
    likes the video.
*   Action Sidebar: A vertical row of buttons on the right side of the screen:
    *   Like (Heart Outline/Filled): Turns red and updates the total like count.
    *   Comment (Speech Bubble): Opens a drawer to read or write comments.
    *   Share (Arrow): Opens your phone's native sharing options to send a link 
        to the video via WhatsApp, text, or social media.
*   User Profile Link: Tapping the creator's profile picture or username 
    navigates directly to their profile page.

C. THE REAL-TIME COMMENT SECTION
*   Sliding Sheet: Tapping the comment button slides up a panel from the 
    bottom of the screen, keeping the video visible in the background.
*   Real-Time Conversations: Using live network communication, new comments 
    appear instantly the moment someone types them on their own phone—no 
    refreshing needed.
*   Easy Navigation: Tapping a commenter's picture or username slides the sheet 
    away and opens their profile.
*   Comment Deletion: You can press and hold on any comment you wrote to delete 
    it. This immediately removes it from the list and decreases the comment 
    counter.

D. SEARCH & DIRECTORY
*   Interactive Search Bar: Search for students by typing their name, @username, 
    or student ID (Matricule).
*   Visual Search Cards: Matching search results display the student's profile 
    picture, @username, masked ID number, and biography.
*   Quick Profile Access: Tapping on any student card navigates to their profile 
    page.

E. STUDENT PROFILES
*   Detailed Info Banner: Displays the student's avatar, username, masked ID, 
    bio, and key stats: the number of videos they have shared, follower count, 
    and how many students they follow.
*   Follow / Unfollow System: Tapping "Follow" subscribes to their posts. 
    If you are already following them, tapping the button prompts a confirmation 
    popup to prevent accidental unfollows.
*   Personal Customization (For your own profile):
    *   Edit Profile: Opens a drawer to write/change your biography, edit your 
        username (checks if the username is available in real-time), and upload 
        a new profile picture from your phone.
    *   Logout: Securely signs you out.
    *   Delete Account: A permanent security action that deletes your entire 
        account, profile, comments, likes, and videos, then logs you out.
*   Video Grid: Your posts are displayed in a clean 3-column thumbnail grid (like 
    Instagram). Tapping any thumbnail opens a full-screen interactive video player.

F. POSTING VIDEOS (THE UPLOAD WIZARD)
*   Video Selection: Choose any video from your phone's photo library.
*   Auto-generated Thumbnail: The app automatically extracts a snapshot from 
    the video to use as a cover image.
*   Caption Composer: Write a caption (up to 200 characters) to describe your 
    post.
*   Upload Safeguards:
    *   File Size Limit: Videos are capped at 70 MB to prevent high data usage 
        and slow loads.
    *   Rate Limiting: Students can post a maximum of 5 videos per hour to 
        prevent spam.
    *   Verification: The app double-checks that the student's database profile 
        is set up correctly before uploading to ensure the post succeeds.
    *   Progress Tracking: A detailed progress bar shows what phase the upload 
        is in (e.g. Uploading video... 45%, Uploading thumbnail..., Saving...).
    *   Cancel Option: You can cancel the upload at any point, which stops all 
        background transfers immediately.
    *   Error Recovery: If your connection drops, the app displays a card with 
        a description of the error and a "Retry" button.


6. INSTANT NOTIFICATION SYSTEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To make interactions feel immediate, U-Buzz utilizes a real-time activity alert 
system:

*   Live Monitoring: The app constantly listens for likes or comments on your 
    videos in the background.
*   Alert Badges: If another user likes or comments on one of your videos, a red 
    badge with the activity count (e.g., "1", "5", or "9+") instantly lights up 
    over the Profile tab on your bottom menu bar.
*   Automatic Reset: Tapping the Profile tab clears your activity count and 
    resets the red badge.
