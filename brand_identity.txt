================================================================================
                           U-BUZZ — BRAND IDENTITY GUIDE
================================================================================

PROJECT NAME: U-Buzz
TAGLINE: IUGET's Campus Network
PRIMARY AUDIENCE: Students of IUGET (Institut Universitaire des Grandes Ecoles des Tropiques), Douala, Cameroon
PLATFORM: iOS & Android (React Native / Expo)
BUNDLE ID: com.ubuzz.campus
LANGUAGE: English (Primary)

================================================================================
                           BRAND POSITIONING
================================================================================

MISSION STATEMENT
U-Buzz is a campus-exclusive, short-form video social platform designed exclusively for 
IUGET students to share, discover, and interact with campus life — solving the 
hyper-local communication gap left by global platforms, while respecting the 
bandwidth constraints and identity privacy needs of students in Cameroon.

BRAND PERSONALITY
- Youthful & Energetic: Matches the TikTok/Reels energy students already love
- Trustworthy & Safe: Campus-only, matricule-verified, privacy-first (masked IDs)
- Smart & Data-Conscious: Built for Cameroon's expensive mobile data reality
- Community-Focused: Not a global feed — a digital campus square
- Modern & Premium: Glassmorphism UI, smooth animations, dark-first aesthetic

UNIQUE VALUE PROPOSITIONS
1. Campus-Exclusive: Content never leaves IUGET — no strangers, no algorithm chasing 
   global virality
2. Data-Conscious Streaming: Cloudinary-powered 1 Mbps / 720p cap saves student data
3. Privacy-First Verification: Matricule-verified (IU****45 masking), honor-system 
   with admin oversight
4. Admin Channel: Official admin announcements with PDF attachments — a dedicated 
   channel that doesn't get buried in the feed
5. Real-Time Campus Pulse: Live comments, likes, and announcement badges — instant 
   campus conversation

- Dark mode ONLY — no light theme
- Primary Purple (#8B5CF6) = Primary CTAs (Upload button, primary actions)

Durations:
- Fast: 120ms — Button presses, icon taps
- Normal: 200ms — Sheet slides, card expansions
- Slow: 300ms — Modal entrances, page transitions
- Skeleton Pulse: 1000ms infinite — Loading placeholders

Key Motions:
- Bottom Sheet: Slide up from bottom (200ms, ease-out)
- Video Swipe: Snap to next (150ms, spring damping 0.8)
- Heart Burst: Scale 0→1.5→1 (300ms, spring) + fade out
- Upload Progress: Linear progress bar, smooth width transition
- Skeleton: Pulse opacity 0.4→1→0.4 (1000ms, ease-in-out)

                           VOICE & TONE
================================================================================

BRAND VOICE ATTRIBUTES
- Friendly but not childish
- Confident but not arrogant
- Inclusive but campus-exclusive
- Smart about data — respectful of constraints
- Clear, direct, jargon-free

TONE SPECTRUM
- Onboarding: Warm, encouraging, guiding ("Welcome to your campus network!")
- Error States: Helpful, non-blaming, actionable ("Connection dropped. Tap to retry.")
- Success: Celebratory but brief ("Posted! Your video is live.")
- Empty States: Inviting, not empty ("No videos yet. Be the first to post!")
- Notifications: Informative, timely ("3 new likes on your video")

- "Campus" = IUGET specifically, not generic
- "Student" or "creator" — never "user" in user-facing copy
- "Matricule" (not "Student ID") — local terminology
- Character limits: Captions 200 chars, Bio 150 chars
- Masked format example: "IU****45" — always 4 asterisks
- No emojis in system copy (UI icons only); students use emojis in their content

================================================================================
                           INTERACTION PATTERNS
================================================================================

NAVIGATION
- Stack navigation within tabs
- Modal sheets for: Comments, Edit Profile, Upload, Search Results detail
- Swipe down to dismiss sheets (except Upload — requires explicit cancel)
- Tab switch: Instant, no animation (standard tab behavior)

GESTURES
- Vertical swipe (feed): Next/Previous video (snap)
- Double tap (video): Like + heart burst
- Long press (video): Report / Delete (owner) menu
- Long press (own comment): Delete confirmation
- Pull to refresh: Feed only (For You / Following)
- Drag handle (sheets): Drag to resize/dismiss

FEEDBACK
- Haptic: Light tap on button press, Medium on double-tap like, Heavy on post success
- Visual: Button press scale 0.96 (120ms), Heart burst, Progress bars, Skeleton loaders
- Audio: None (app is mute-default, user controls volume)

LOADING STATES
- Skeleton: Gradient Surface pulse (1000ms) — Feed cards, Profile grid, Search results
- Inline: Spinner 20px (Primary Purple) — Button submissions, Upload progress
- Full-screen: Only on initial auth load — Logo + Gradient Primary pulse

================================================================================
                           CONTENT GUIDELINES
================================================================================

VIDEO SPECS (Enforced Client-Side)
- Max Duration: 60 seconds
- Max File Size: 30 MB (70 MB in some builds — enforce 30 MB)
- Format: MP4, MOV (Cloudinary transcodes to f_auto)
- Orientation: Vertical (9:16) preferred, any accepted
- Thumbnail: Auto-generated at 1s mark (expo-video-thumbnails)

CAPTIONS
- Max 200 characters
- Sanitized: HTML tags stripped
- Hashtags: Not parsed as links (MVP) — plain text
- Mentions: Not parsed (MVP) — plain text

PROFILES
- Username: @ + alphanumeric + underscore, 3-20 chars, unique
- Display Name: 1-30 chars
- Bio: Max 150 chars, plain text
- Avatar: Square, min 100x100, max 5MB, Cloudinary f_auto,q_auto,w_100,c_fill,g_face

ANNOUNCEMENTS (Admin Only)
- Title: Max 100 chars
- Body: Max 2000 chars
- Image: Optional, same as video thumbnail specs
- Document: PDF/TXT, max 10MB, Cloudinary raw upload

================================================================================
                           ACCESSIBILITY
================================================================================

- Dark mode only — ensure 4.5:1 contrast minimum (WCAG AA)
- Text Primary (#FFFFFF) on Surface (#16161E) = 15.3:1 ✓
- Text Secondary (#A1A1AA) on Surface = 7.2:1 ✓
- Primary Purple (#8B5CF6) on White = 4.6:1 ✓ (large text)
- Pink (#EC4899) on White = 4.1:1 ⚠ (use on dark only, or bold)
- Touch targets: Minimum 44x44pt (iOS) / 48x48dp (Android)
- Semantic labels on all icon-only buttons
- Dynamic Type: Support system font scaling (Body Large → up to 24px)
- Reduce Motion: Respect prefers-reduced-motion (disable heart burst, skeleton pulse)
- Screen Reader: Test with VoiceOver / TalkBack — all interactive elements labeled

================================================================================
                           PLATFORM ADAPTATIONS
================================================================================

iOS (Expo)
- Safe Area: Top (Dynamic Island/notch), Bottom (Home indicator)
- SecureStore: Native, 2048 byte limit → ChunkedSecureStoreAdapter required
- Video Player: expo-video (AVPlayer backend)
- Haptics: expo-haptics (impactLight, impactMedium, impactHeavy)
- Share Sheet: Native iOS share (expo-sharing)
- Keyboard: Avoids bottom sheets automatically

ANDROID (Expo)
- SecureStore: 2048 byte limit → ChunkedSecureStoreAdapter required
- Video Player: expo-video (Media3/ExoPlayer backend)
- Back Gesture: System back dismisses sheets/modals
- Edge-to-Edge: Enabled, status bar translucent
- Keyboard: WindowSoftInputMode.adjustResize for sheets

WEB (Not Supported in MVP)
- Responsive breakpoints not required
- No PWA manifest needed

- Icon Only: Stylized play button in Gradient Primary circle
- Horizontal: Icon + Wordmark (24px gap)
- Minimum Size: 24px height (icon), 120px width (wordmark)
- Clear Space: 1x icon height on all sides

LOGO RESTRICTIONS
- NO MODIFICATIONS: Do not stretch, rotate, skew, recolor, or apply effects (shadows, gradients, outlines) to the logo
- NO RECONSTRUCTION: Do not recreate the logo from scratch; always use provided SVG/PNG assets
- NO CONTAINED SHAPES: Do not place the logo inside other shapes (circles, squares, badges) unless using the provided Icon Only variant
- NO TAGLINE LOCKUP: Do not lock "IUGET's Campus Network" or any tagline to the logo — use separately with 32px minimum spacing
- NO CO-LOCATION WITH OTHER LOGOS: Do not place U-Buzz logo directly adjacent to partner/institutional logos without a 2x clear space divider
- MINIMUM CONTRAST: Logo must meet 4.5:1 contrast against background; use Icon Only (white) on dark media, Primary (gradient) on light media
- BACKGROUND RESTRICTIONS: 
  * Primary logo: Only on Brand Dark (#0A0A0F), Surface (#16161E), or pure white
  * Icon Only: On Gradient Primary, Brand Dark, or pure white — never on photos, patterns, or low-contrast colors
- PROHIBITED USES:
  * As watermark on user-generated content
  * In user profiles, video overlays, or comments
  * Merchandise without written brand approval
  * Modified for holidays/events (no Santa hats, flag overlays, etc.)
  * Animated unless using approved motion system (200ms ease-out scale on tap)
- APP ICON ONLY: The app icon (1024x1024, adaptive icon) is a separate asset — do not use it as marketing logo
- ATTRIBUTION: When used in press/partnerships, include "U-Buzz — IUGET's Campus Network" text nearby
- ASSET SOURCE: Always pull from /brand-assets/ (SVG for print, PNG @2x/@3x for digital) — no screenshots from app
